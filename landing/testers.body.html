Help us get on Google Play.

Before a new app can be listed on Google Play, Google requires at least 12 people to test it for 14 days. We need your help to get there.

What you'd do

  1. Leave your Google email address below. It's the address you use for the Play Store on your phone, usually a Gmail address.
  2. We'll email you a link once the test opens.
  3. Install the app from Google Play through that link, and keep it on your phone for 14 days. Open it now and then, look around, and vote if you like.

You need an Android phone. It's free, and you can tell us what you think any time.

Sign up to test

We use your address only to add you to the test and send you the link. It's never put on the public record, and we delete it after the test. See our privacy policy.
