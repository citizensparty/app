Help us test

Help us get on Google Play.

Google won't list a new app until 12 people have tested it for 14 days. It takes about 5 minutes, and then you just keep the app on your phone.

Easier on your phone. Scan this to continue there.

Step 1 of 4

Find the email you use for Google Play.

  1. Open the Play Store on your phone.
  2. Tap your picture in the top right corner.
  3. Your email is under your name. Remember it. You'll use it in the next steps.

More than one email? Tap the little arrow to see them all, and pick the one you want to use.

Step 2 of 4

Join the Google group

  1. Go here →
  2. Tap Join group.
  3. If you don't see it, tap Sign in first.

We never share your email, and other testers can't see it.

Step 3 of 4

Join on Google Play

  1. Go to Google Play →
  2. Tap Become a tester.

If it says the app isn't available, Google is still adding you to the group. Wait 10 minutes and try again.

Step 4 of 4

Install the app.

  1. Open Citizens Party on Google Play →
  2. Tap Install.
  3. Keep it installed for at least 14 days

See “Item not found”? The Play Store is signed in with a different email from the one you used to join. Tap your picture in the Play Store, switch to the email from step 1, and try again.

Thank you. You're helping build a party that its members run.
