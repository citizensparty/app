Get the app

On your phone, in a minute.

Members take part through the Citizens Party app. It makes your key, keeps your copy of the record and signs your votes. Your key lives only on this device. If you lose it, you'll re-register with a new one.

We're in open beta, so setup takes a few more steps than it will later.

Android

{{APK}}
  1. Tap the download button, then open the file.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. If Play Protect says the app is blocked, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Tap Install, then open Citizens Party.

Coming to Google Play soon.

iPhone

The iPhone app is coming to the App Store soon. Until then, use the web app; it works the same way.

Open the app in Safari
  1. Open the link in Safari (not Chrome: only Safari can add it to your home screen).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Citizens Party from your home screen. Keep it there: iPhones can clear a website's data after a week without use, but not a home screen app's.

Computer

Open the web app

Works in any up-to-date browser. Chrome and Edge can install it like an app.
